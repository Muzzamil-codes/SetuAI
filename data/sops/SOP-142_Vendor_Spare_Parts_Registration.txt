STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

SOP Number: SOP-142
Title: Vendor-Supplied Spare Parts Registration and Traceability
Revision: 05
Effective Date: 15-Sep-2024
Prepared By: M. Solanki, Maintenance Planning Engineer
Approved By: N. Krishnan, Chief Engineer
Review Cycle: 24 months

1. PURPOSE
To ensure every vendor-supplied spare part installed on critical equipment is logged
with full traceability — vendor, part batch, installation date, and installing
equipment tag — so that recurring failures can be traced back to a specific supply
batch during root cause analysis.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all spare parts sourced from empanelled vendors for use on Unit-1 and
Unit-2 critical valves, pumps, and instrumentation.

3. REGISTRATION LOG (Unit-2 Valve Components — Extract)
    Entry 2023-11: Vendor XYZ Engineering Pvt. Ltd. supplied replacement gland packing

        Shift-B. Installation followed a DEVIATION finding logged under SOP-089 on

    Entry 2024-06: Vendor XYZ Engineering Pvt. Ltd. supplied a second batch of

        installed 02-Jun-2024, following a repeat DEVIATION finding. Root cause review
        (RCA-2024-014) attributed the repeat failure to a batch-level material defect

    Entry 2024-09: Vendor ABC Instrumentation Ltd. supplied a replacement pressure

    Entry 2025-02: Vendor XYZ Engineering Pvt. Ltd. supplied a control valve actuator
        seal kit (Part No. AS-211-K) for Valve V-211, installed 10-Feb-2025.

4. PROCEDURE
4.1 On receipt of any vendor-supplied spare part, the maintenance planning engineer
    logs the vendor name, part number, batch number (where applicable), and intended
    installation location in the Registration Log before the part is released to the
    maintenance crew.
4.2 On installation, the maintenance crew confirms the installed part number matches
    the logged entry and records the actual installation date.
4.3 Where a component fails within 12 months of installation, the maintenance planning
    engineer must check the Registration Log for other equipment that received parts
    from the same vendor batch, and flag them for proactive inspection per SOP-089.
4.4 Vendors with two or more batch-level defects within a 24-month period are placed
    under review by the Procurement Committee and may be suspended from the empanelled
    vendor list pending a corrective action report.
