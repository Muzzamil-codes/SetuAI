BHARAT REFINERIES LIMITED (BRL) — INSTRUMENTATION & CALIBRATION LAB
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

SOP Number: SOP-055
Title: Instrument Calibration, Certification and Stamp Validity
Revision: 07
Effective Date: 01-Apr-2025
Prepared By: P. Ramesh, Calibration Lab In-Charge
Approved By: S. Deshmukh, Head of Process Safety
Review Cycle: 12 months

1. PURPOSE
To define the calibration frequency, certification stamping procedure, and validity
period for field pressure gauges, thermocouples, and flow meters used in Unit-1 and
Unit-2, and to define how calibration validity is checked during inspection rounds.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all portable and fixed field instruments requiring periodic calibration
under the BRL Instrumentation Register.

3. CALIBRATION FREQUENCY AND STAMPING
3.1 All pressure gauges used for valve tolerance checks under SOP-114 must be
    calibrated at intervals not exceeding 6 months.
3.2 On successful calibration, the Calibration Lab applies a physical stamp to the
    instrument body and to the associated inspection card, showing the calibration
    date and the lab technician's registration code. The stamp remains valid for
    exactly 6 months from the date shown, after which the instrument must be
    withdrawn from service until recalibrated.
3.3 The calibration date stamp format is DD-MM-YY, applied using the standard BRL
    embossing stamp. Where a stamp is applied to a physical inspection card rather
    than the instrument itself, care must be taken during field use to avoid smudging,
    since a smudged or partially legible date cannot be used to confirm calibration
    validity.

4. HANDLING UNREADABLE OR DISPUTED STAMPS
4.1 If a calibration stamp on an inspection card is smudged, partially obscured, or
    otherwise not confidently legible, the instrument's calibration status must be
    treated as UNKNOWN, not assumed valid and not assumed expired.
4.2 An UNKNOWN calibration status must be resolved by cross-checking the Calibration
    Lab's digital register (which records every calibration event independently of the
    physical stamp) before any reading taken with that instrument is used in a
    Deviation Flag determination or an Approval Note.
4.3 Under no circumstances should a technician, engineer, or automated system estimate
    or infer a calibration date from context (e.g. "likely calibrated during the last
    turnaround") in place of a confirmed stamp or register entry. Any finding relying
    on an instrument with UNKNOWN calibration status must be explicitly marked as such
    in the inspection record and in any resulting Approval Note per SOP-301.

5. REFERENCE STANDARDS
5.1 Internal: SOP-114 (Valve Pressure Tolerance), SOP-089 (Inspection & Findings
    Reporting), SOP-301 (Approval Note Drafting & Sign-off)

6. REVISION HISTORY
Rev 05 (2023): Reduced calibration interval from 12 months to 6 months for pressure
    gauges following a drift-related incident.
Rev 06 (2024): Introduced parallel digital register requirement.
Rev 07 (2025): Added Section 4 (unreadable stamp handling) after a near-miss where a
    smudged calibration date was misread as valid during a manual review.
