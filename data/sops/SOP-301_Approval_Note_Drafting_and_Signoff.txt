STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

SOP Number: SOP-301
Title: Approval Note (Note for Approval / NFA) Drafting and Sign-off Procedure
Revision: 04
Effective Date: 10-Jun-2025
Prepared By: K. Menon, Documentation Officer
Approved By: N. Krishnan, Chief Engineer
Review Cycle: 24 months

1. PURPOSE
To standardize the structure and mandatory content of Approval Notes (NFAs) raised
following inspection findings, vendor evaluations, or engineering calculations
requiring management sign-off.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all Approval Notes raised across Unit-1 and Unit-2 for maintenance,
procurement, and engineering matters.

3. MANDATORY STRUCTURE
Every Approval Note must contain, in this order:
    3.1 Header Block — NFA number, date, originating division, equipment tag(s)
        involved, and classification (Routine / Deviation / Critical) per SOP-089.
    3.2 Background — a concise statement of what triggered the note (e.g. a specific
        inspection finding, with reference to the source inspection card date and
        inspector).
    3.3 Findings — the specific readings, observations, or calculation results being
        reported, each traceable to its source (inspection card, calculation
        worksheet, or vendor document). Any finding that could not be independently
        verified must be marked "UNVERIFIED" per SOP-089 Section 3.5 and SOP-055
        Section 4, and must never be presented with the same confidence as a verified
        finding.
    3.4 Recommendation — the proposed corrective action or decision being requested.
    3.5 Approval Block — named designations required to sign off, in sequence: Shift
        Supervisor, Section Engineer, Head of Process Safety (for Deviation/Critical
        classifications only).

4. FORMATTING REQUIREMENTS
4.1 Approval Notes are issued as Word (.docx) documents using the standard BRL NFA
    template, not as email text or informal memos.
4.2 Any referenced scanned document (inspection card, vendor certificate) must be
    embedded as an image crop within the Findings section, not merely described in
    text, so that the approving authority can visually verify the source.
4.3 Numeric findings derived from engineering calculations (e.g. under SOP-207) must
    show the full calculation steps in an appendix, not just the final result.

5. REFERENCE STANDARDS
5.1 Internal: SOP-089 (Inspection & Findings Reporting), SOP-207 (Wall Thickness
    Compliance), SOP-055 (Instrument Calibration & Certification), SOP-142 (Vendor
    Spare Parts Registration)

6. REVISION HISTORY
Rev 02 (2021): Mandated embedded image crops in place of text descriptions.
Rev 03 (2023): Added mandatory calculation appendix requirement.
Rev 04 (2025): Added explicit "UNVERIFIED" marking requirement in Section 3.3,
    aligned with the corresponding update to SOP-089 and SOP-055.
