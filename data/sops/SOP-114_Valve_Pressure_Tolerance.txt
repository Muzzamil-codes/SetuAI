BHARAT REFINERIES LIMITED (BRL) — UNIT-2 PROCESS SAFETY DIVISION
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

SOP Number: SOP-114
Title: Valve Pressure Tolerance, Testing and Acceptance Criteria
Revision: 04
Effective Date: 12-Jan-2025
Prepared By: R. Iyer, Sr. Process Engineer
Approved By: S. Deshmukh, Head of Process Safety
Review Cycle: 24 months

1. PURPOSE
This procedure defines the acceptable pressure tolerance band for control and isolation
valves in hydrocarbon service across Unit-1 and Unit-2, and the testing method used to
confirm a valve is fit for continued service during scheduled and unscheduled inspections.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all gate, globe, and control valves rated Class 300 and above installed in
Unit-2 process piping, including but not limited to Valve V-201 through V-220.

3. DEFINITIONS
3.1 Rated Tolerance — the maximum permissible deviation between the observed field
    reading and the valve's design set-point, expressed in bar.
3.2 Deviation Flag — a recorded finding where the observed reading exceeds the rated
    tolerance and requires supervisor sign-off before the valve is returned to service.

4. PRESSURE TOLERANCE TABLE (Unit-2 Valves)
    Valve V-204 (Gate Valve, Class 300, Line 2-PL-114): Rated tolerance ±0.10 bar
        against a design set-point of 4.0 bar. Field readings between 3.90 and 4.10 bar
        are considered acceptable without further action. Readings between 4.10 and
        4.25 bar are logged as a Deviation Flag requiring supervisor review but do not
        require immediate isolation. Readings above 4.25 bar require immediate
        isolation per SOP-089 Section 6.
    Valve V-207 (Globe Valve, Class 300, Line 2-PL-118): Rated tolerance ±0.15 bar
        against a design set-point of 6.5 bar.
    Valve V-211 (Control Valve, Class 600, Line 2-PL-122): Rated tolerance ±0.08 bar
        against a design set-point of 9.0 bar.

5. PROCEDURE
5.1 Field technician records the gauge reading during scheduled rounds using the
    calibrated pressure gauge assigned per SOP-055.
5.2 Reading is compared against the Pressure Tolerance Table in Section 4.
5.3 If within rated tolerance, technician initials the inspection card and no further
    action is required.
5.4 If a Deviation Flag is raised, the technician notifies the shift supervisor within
    30 minutes and logs the finding per SOP-089.
5.5 Deviation Flags on Valve V-204 specifically require cross-verification against the
    vendor-supplied component history (see SOP-142) before sign-off, since the last two
    recorded deviations on V-204 were traced to a worn gland packing rather than a
    genuine set-point drift.

6. REFERENCE STANDARDS
6.1 ASME B31.3 — Process Piping
6.2 API 598 — Valve Inspection and Testing
6.3 Internal: SOP-089 (Inspection & Findings Reporting), SOP-142 (Vendor-Supplied
    Spare Parts Registration), SOP-055 (Instrument Calibration & Certification)
