BHARAT REFINERIES LIMITED (BRL) — UNIT-2 OPERATIONS DIVISION
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

SOP Number: SOP-089
Title: Periodic Inspection Rounds and Findings Reporting
Revision: 06
Effective Date: 20-Jul-2025
Prepared By: T. Bhatt, Shift Operations Lead
Approved By: S. Deshmukh, Head of Process Safety
Review Cycle: 12 months

1. PURPOSE
To standardize how field technicians record inspection findings during routine and
turnaround inspections, and how those findings are escalated into a formal Approval
Note (NFA) when corrective action or management sign-off is required.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all shift-based and turnaround inspection rounds in Unit-2, covering
pressure vessels, valves, piping, and rotating equipment.

3. PROCEDURE
3.1 Inspection Sheet Format. Every inspection round is recorded on the standard BRL
    inspection card, either in physical carbon-copy form or via the digital tablet
    application. Each entry must capture: equipment tag, reading/observation, date,
    inspector initials, and a supervisor stamp for any reading outside normal range.
3.2 Handwritten Entries. Where physical inspection cards are used, handwriting must be
    in block capitals for equipment tags and numeric readings to reduce transcription
    error during digitization. Supervisor approval stamps are applied directly on the
    card next to the flagged reading.
3.3 Findings Classification. Each finding is classified as:
        NORMAL — reading within tolerance, no action required.
        DEVIATION — reading outside tolerance per the relevant equipment SOP (e.g.
            SOP-114 for valve pressure), requiring supervisor review within one shift.
        CRITICAL — reading indicating an immediate safety risk, requiring immediate
            isolation and escalation to the Head of Process Safety.
3.4 Escalation to Approval Note. Any DEVIATION or CRITICAL finding that results in a
    corrective action, a component replacement, or a change to operating parameters
    must be drafted into a formal Approval Note (NFA) per SOP-301 within 48 hours of
    the finding being logged. The Approval Note must reference the original inspection
    card (including a legible image or scan of the card) and must not paraphrase the
    original reading without preserving traceability back to the source entry.
3.5 Illegible or Ambiguous Entries. If a reading, date, or stamp on a physical
    inspection card cannot be confidently read during digitization (e.g. due to
    smudging, water damage, or unclear handwriting), it must be marked "UNVERIFIED —
    CONFIRM WITH ORIGINATING SHIFT" rather than estimated or guessed, and the
    originating shift supervisor must be contacted before the finding is finalized in
    any Approval Note.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES
4.1 Field Technician — records readings accurately, flags anything outside normal
    range immediately rather than at end of shift.
4.2 Shift Supervisor — reviews all DEVIATION and CRITICAL findings within the
    timeframes in Section 3.3, applies approval stamp.
4.3 Documentation Officer — digitizes physical inspection cards within 24 hours,
    maintains the digital archive, and is responsible for applying the
    "UNVERIFIED" marking described in Section 3.5 where applicable.

5. REFERENCE STANDARDS
5.1 Internal: SOP-114 (Valve Pressure Tolerance), SOP-301 (Approval Note Drafting &
    Sign-off), SOP-055 (Instrument Calibration & Certification)

6. REVISION HISTORY
Rev 04 (2022): Introduced digital tablet application as alternative to carbon-copy
    cards.
Rev 05 (2024): Added CRITICAL classification tier following an incident review.
Rev 06 (2025): Added Section 3.5 (illegible entry handling) after a near-miss caused
    by a mis-transcribed calibration date on a smudged inspection card.
