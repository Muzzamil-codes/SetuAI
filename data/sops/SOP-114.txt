Standard Operating Procedure SOP-114: Unit-2 Valve Specifications.
The designated main line feed contains Valve V-204.
Valve V-204 nominal operating pressure is 4.0 bar.
Valve V-204 rated tolerance is ±0.1 bar per SOP-114. Any reading between 3.9 bar and 4.1 bar is normal.
Emergency shutdown trigger: Pressure exceeding 5.0 bar.
Inspection frequency: Bi-weekly by certified technician.
