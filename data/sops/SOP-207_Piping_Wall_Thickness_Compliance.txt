BHARAT REFINERIES LIMITED (BRL) — ENGINEERING & INTEGRITY DIVISION
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

SOP Number: SOP-207
Title: Minimum Wall Thickness Calculation and Compliance Verification for Process Piping
Revision: 03
Effective Date: 03-Mar-2024
Prepared By: A. Fernandes, Piping Integrity Engineer
Approved By: N. Krishnan, Chief Engineer
Review Cycle: 36 months

1. PURPOSE
To establish the mandatory calculation method and acceptance criteria for verifying
that installed or proposed process piping meets minimum wall thickness requirements
under internal design pressure, in accordance with ASME B31.3.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all carbon steel and low-alloy process piping in Unit-1 and Unit-2 operating
above 10 bar internal pressure. Excludes utility piping (air, water, nitrogen) rated
below 10 bar, which is covered under SOP-208.

3. CALCULATION METHOD
3.1 The minimum required wall thickness (t) shall be calculated using the ASME B31.3
    straight pipe under internal pressure formula:

        t = (P * D) / (2 * (S * E * W + P * Y))

    where:
        P = internal design pressure (bar, converted to consistent units)
        D = outside diameter of pipe (mm)
        S = allowable stress for the pipe material at design temperature (from
            ASME B31.3 Table A-1)
        E = quality factor (weld joint efficiency; use 1.0 for seamless pipe)
        W = weld joint strength reduction factor (use 1.0 unless otherwise specified)
        Y = coefficient from ASME B31.3 Table 304.1.1, dependent on material and
            temperature (use 0.4 for ferritic steels below 480°C)

3.2 A corrosion allowance of 1.5 mm shall be added to the calculated minimum thickness
    for all sour and wet H2S service lines, and 1.0 mm for all other hydrocarbon
    service lines, per BRL internal corrosion allowance policy CA-02.

3.3 The final required thickness (t_required) is the calculated t plus the applicable
    corrosion allowance, rounded up to the nearest standard commercial pipe schedule.

4. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
4.1 Installed pipe nominal wall thickness must be equal to or greater than t_required.
4.2 Any calculation resulting in a t_required that does not correspond to a standard
    commercial schedule must be escalated to the Chief Engineer for a special-order
    pipe review before procurement.
4.3 All wall-thickness calculations submitted for an approval note must show the full
    formula substitution step-by-step, not just the final result, per BRL documentation
    policy DOC-11.

5. WORKED REFERENCE EXAMPLE (for training purposes only — not a substitute for a
   project-specific calculation)
   For a carbon steel line at 45 bar design pressure, 168.3 mm OD, allowable stress
   138 MPa, seamless (E=1.0), Y=0.4:
        t = (45 * 168.3) / (2 * (138 * 1.0 * 1.0 + 45 * 0.4)) ≈ 5.08 mm
   Adding a 1.0 mm corrosion allowance gives a required thickness of approximately
   6.08 mm, which would typically map to Schedule 40 or higher depending on the
   specific pipe size table in use.

6. REFERENCE STANDARDS
6.1 ASME B31.3 — Process Piping, Chapter II, Part 3 (Pressure Design of Piping

6.2 Internal: CA-02 (Corrosion Allowance Policy), DOC-11 (Calculation Documentation
