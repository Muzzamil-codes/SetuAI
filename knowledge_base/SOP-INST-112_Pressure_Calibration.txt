STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Document ID: SOP-INST-112
Revision: 2.0
Date Effective: 2023-08-22
Department: Instrumentation & Control
Title: Calibration of Smart Pressure Transmitters (HART Protocol)

1. PURPOSE
To standardize the calibration procedure for pressure and differential pressure transmitters to ensure accurate data transmission to the DCS (Distributed Control System).

2. REQUIRED TOOLS
- Fluke 754 Documenting Process Calibrator (or equivalent)
- HART Communicator
- Deadweight tester or precision pressure pump
- Multimeter

3. CALIBRATION TOLERANCES
3.1. Standard Process Transmitters: ±0.25% of calibrated span.
3.2. Custody Transfer Transmitters: ±0.1% of calibrated span.
3.3. Output signal acceptable range: 4.00 mA to 20.00 mA (±0.02 mA).

4. PROCEDURE
4.1. Coordinate with the Control Room Operator to put the control loop in MANUAL mode.
4.2. Isolate the transmitter from the process by closing the block valves on the instrument manifold.
4.3. Vent the pressure safely using the bleed valves.
4.4. Connect the HART communicator and record the "As Found" values (Zero and Span).
4.5. Connect the precision pressure pump and apply 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of the span.
4.6. Record the mA output at each step.
4.7. If the deviation at any point exceeds the tolerances defined in Section 3, perform a sensor trim and output trim using the HART communicator.
4.8. Record the "As Left" values.

5. REPORTING
All calibration certificates must be uploaded to the Asset Management System. If a transmitter fails calibration and cannot be trimmed, it must be tagged as "Out of Service" and a replacement must be drafted.
