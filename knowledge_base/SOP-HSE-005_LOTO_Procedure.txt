Department: Health, Safety & Environment (HSE)
Title: Lockout / Tagout (LOTO) Procedures for Hazardous Energy Control

1. PURPOSE
This procedure establishes the minimum requirements for the lockout/tagout of energy-isolating devices to protect employees from hazardous energy during maintenance operations.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all mechanical, electrical, pneumatic, hydraulic, and chemical energy sources within the refinery premises.

3. DEFINITIONS
3.1. Authorized Employee: A person who locks out machines to perform maintenance.
3.2. Affected Employee: An employee whose job requires them to operate a machine on which maintenance is being performed.
3.3. Positive Isolation: Using a physical barrier, such as a blind flange or slip blind, to completely cut off flow.

4. ENERGY ISOLATION PROCEDURE
4.1. Preparation: The authorized employee must identify all types and magnitudes of energy associated with the equipment.
4.2. Shutdown: Turn off the equipment using normal stopping procedures (e.g., stop buttons, toggle switches).
4.3. Isolation: Physically operate the energy-isolating devices (breakers, valves) so the equipment is completely separated from its energy source.
4.4. Lockout application: Apply individual locks and danger tags to each isolating device. Every worker involved must place their personal lock.
4.5. Stored Energy Release: Relieve, disconnect, or restrain any stored energy (e.g., bleeding pressure from lines, discharging capacitors, blocking elevated parts).
4.6. Verification (Try-Out): Ensure no personnel are exposed, then attempt to start the equipment using the normal operating controls to verify it will not start. Return the controls to "OFF" after verification.

5. REMOVAL OF LOTO
5.1. Inspect the work area to ensure non-essential items have been removed and machine components are intact.
5.2. Ensure all employees are safely positioned.
5.3. Remove lockout devices and tags. Only the employee who applied a lock may remove it.
5.4. Notify affected employees that maintenance is complete and equipment is ready for use.
